# A0-A6 Regime Taxonomy

VEHICLE-SUPRA classifies nodes according to internal and relational tension.

| Regime | Name | Interpretation | Prototype response |
|---|---|---|---|
| A0 | Stable | Low internal and relational tension. | Normal operation. |
| A1 | External pressure | Relational pressure is rising. | Monitor, validate inputs, and increase observation. |
| A2 | Recovering | The node is returning toward admissible coherence. | Continue verification and stabilize. |
| A3 | Internal incoherence | Internal dimensions are out of balance. | Apply projection-governed recovery. |
| A4 | Isolated | The node has no active relational connections. | Standby, inspection, or controlled reintegration. |
| A5 | Crisis | High internal incoherence or non-convergent behavior. | Escalate recovery in simulation. |
| A6 | Cascade | Multiple nodes show simultaneous instability. | Isolate propagation paths and rebuild from coherent nodes. |

The thresholds used for classification are configurable through `SupraNodeConfig`.
